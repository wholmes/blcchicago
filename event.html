BLC Lakeside Chat with Mayor Brandon Johnson

Join BLC members and civic leaders for a conversation on Chicago’s economy, Black business, and community investment.

About this event

The Business Leadership Council hosts Mayor Brandon Johnson for a Lakeside Chat—a candid dialogue with Chicago’s Black business leaders on growth, procurement, and building generational wealth across our neighborhoods.

This event is open to the public. Anyone can view event details and learn more. Ticket purchases are reserved for BLC members in good standing, who may buy tickets for themselves and for guests.

What to expect

Networking lunch, moderated Q&A, and time to connect with fellow BLC members and civic partners. Business attire encouraged.
