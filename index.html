Who we are

Power. Values. Purpose.

BLC leadership →

Power

Chicago’s premier Black business organization—representing leaders and entrepreneurs in positions of influence across corporate and civic leadership.

Harnessing the power of today’s Black business leaders to build the next generation of Chicago’s business leaders.

Values

The Business Leadership Council builds bridges in every arena as a strong foundation for business growth and development within the Black community.

We are a values-driven organization, committed to making a positive impact on our communities.

Purpose

The mentor and protégé paradigm needs to be reimagined—having Black companies participate as primes and mentor emerging Black companies as protégés.
