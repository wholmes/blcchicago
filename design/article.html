Reimagining the mentor–protégé paradigm

Black companies participating as primes—and mentoring emerging Black companies as protégés.

The mentor and protégé paradigm needs to be reimagined by having Black companies participate as primes and mentor emerging Black companies as protégés.

That shift is more than language. It asks established firms to open delivery capacity, share relationships, and build a pipeline that compounds across Chicago’s Black business community.

BLC exists to make those bridges practical—through the directory, member connections, and a calendar that keeps leaders in the same room.
