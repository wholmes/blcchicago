Our goals

Why we lead

Sustainability

To improve the well-being and long-term sustainability of the Black community, our businesses must participate in the modern economy. We aim to create generational wealth from the ground up.

Value

To increase appreciation of the capacity of Black businesses, we must demonstrate our ability to deliver value to governments, institutions, and corporations.

Membership

Celebrate strengths. Share insights. Plan for future success.

As a member of the BLC you gain directory visibility, member connections, and a seat at Chicago’s premier Black business table.
